**Crypto launchpad development is building the system that lets your exchange host token sales — an IEO platform where new projects raise funds and your users get early access.** Done well, a launchpad drives sign-ups, deposits, and trading volume for the tokens it launches. Here's what building one involves and the risks that come with it.

## What a launchpad does

A launchpad (or IEO platform) is a curated venue where vetted projects sell their tokens to your users before those tokens trade publicly. The exchange acts as gatekeeper and distributor: it screens projects, runs the sale, distributes tokens, and usually [lists the token](/blog/listing-tokens-and-trading-pairs) for trading afterward ([Antier](https://www.antiersolutions.com/blogs/crypto-launchpad-development-company/)). For operators, it's a growth and revenue engine — new projects bring their communities to *your* platform.

## What launchpad development involves

- **Project onboarding & vetting** — application, due-diligence, and approval workflows (your reputation rides on the quality of what you list).
- **Sale mechanics** — allocation models (first-come, lottery, tiered by your token/staking), caps, and vesting schedules.
- **Token distribution & vesting** — smart-contract or custodial release of tokens over time.
- **User tiers & eligibility** — often gated by holding or staking your platform token.
- **Compliance** — token sales can be securities offerings in many jurisdictions; KYC and legal review are essential ([Dappfort](https://www.dappfort.com/blog/crypto-launchpad-development/)).

## Why operators add one

A launchpad compounds the [core exchange revenue model](/blog/how-crypto-exchanges-make-money): you earn from the sale itself, from the trading volume of newly listed tokens, and from the deposits and sign-ups each project's community brings. It's a strong [go-to-market](/blog/go-to-market-new-crypto-exchange) lever for a growing venue.

<Callout title="The reputational risk is the real cost">
A launchpad is only as trusted as the projects it lists. One rug-pull or failed token damages the credibility of your whole exchange. Rigorous vetting and clear disclosures aren't optional — they're the product. Build the diligence process before the tech.
</Callout>

## The takeaway

Crypto launchpad development gives your exchange a token-sale engine that drives users, deposits, and volume — but its value is inseparable from the quality of your vetting. The technical build (sale mechanics, vesting, tiers) is the easy part; the diligence and compliance that protect your reputation are what actually make a launchpad worth having.
