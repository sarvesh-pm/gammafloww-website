**A centralized exchange (CEX) is operated by a company that runs the order book and custodies user funds, while a decentralized exchange (DEX) runs on smart contracts, letting users trade directly from their own wallets without handing over custody.** The trade-off is roughly custody and control versus speed and features. Here's how the two models actually differ.

## The core difference: custody

On a CEX, you deposit funds and the platform holds them for you — you trade against its order book and trust it to safeguard assets and honor withdrawals. On a DEX, you keep your assets in your own wallet and trades settle on-chain via smart contracts; the exchange never takes custody ([Gemini](https://www.gemini.com/cryptopedia/decentralized-exchange-crypto-dex)). "Not your keys, not your coins" is the DEX pitch; "someone to call, and a fast, familiar product" is the CEX pitch.

## Where each model wins

**CEX strengths:**
- **Performance** — an off-chain [matching engine](/blog/inside-a-crypto-matching-engine) delivers low latency and deep [liquidity](/blog/crypto-exchange-liquidity-solutions).
- **Features** — advanced order types, fiat on-ramps, and sophisticated [derivatives](/blog/perp-dex-vs-centralized-perpetuals).
- **Support & recovery** — customer service, and a path back if you lose access.

**DEX strengths:**
- **Self-custody** — you hold your keys; no exchange can freeze or lose your funds.
- **Permissionless access** — no account or KYC to start trading (though this varies by jurisdiction).
- **Transparency** — settlement is on-chain and auditable.

**DEX trade-offs:** on-chain settlement can be slower and costlier (gas), the UX is less forgiving, and a mistake is usually irreversible.

## Derivatives: the practical dividing line

For **derivatives specifically**, centralized venues still dominate on liquidity and product depth, though on-chain "perp DEXs" have grown fast. The choice mirrors the broader one: a perp DEX gives you self-custody and on-chain transparency, a centralized venue gives you speed, depth, and a fuller product. We compare them directly in [Perp DEX vs. Centralized Perpetuals](/blog/perp-dex-vs-centralized-perpetuals).

<Callout title="It's a spectrum, not a binary">
Many venues blend the models — centralized matching with on-chain settlement, or DEXs with off-chain order books. And a CEX isn't the same as a [broker](/blog/crypto-broker-vs-exchange). The real questions are: who holds custody, where does settlement happen, and what are you trading?
</Callout>

## The takeaway

CEX vs. DEX comes down to custody and control versus speed and features. A CEX holds your funds and gives you a fast, full-featured, supported product; a DEX lets you keep your keys and trade permissionlessly, at some cost to speed and ease. For derivatives, centralized venues still lead on depth — but the on-chain alternative is real and growing. Pick the model whose trade-offs match how you trade.
