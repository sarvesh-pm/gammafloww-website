If you're buying rather than building a derivatives venue, the provider you choose determines your matching performance, your liquidity, your compliance posture and your economics for years. This is a neutral guide to how to compare the field — and the providers most often shortlisted in 2026. (Pair it with our [buyer's checklist](/blog/how-to-choose-white-label-exchange-provider) and [vendor due-diligence guide](/blog/white-label-exchange-vendor-due-diligence).)

## How to compare providers (before the names)

Ranking "the best" in the abstract is meaningless — the right provider depends on your market, product and constraints. Score each on:

- **Engine & performance** — matching latency and throughput under load, not on a slide (see [inside a matching engine](/blog/inside-a-crypto-matching-engine)).
- **Derivatives depth** — perps, dated futures, options; margin modes; collateral options; a real [risk and liquidation engine](/blog/risk-and-liquidation-engines).
- **Liquidity** — how the provider solves the [cold-start problem](/blog/crypto-exchange-liquidity-cold-start): aggregated books, market-making, shared liquidity.
- **Custody & security** — key management, cold storage, insurance, and integrations.
- **Compliance tooling** — KYC/AML, surveillance, and support for your jurisdiction.
- **Pricing model** — setup vs. revenue share vs. SaaS; transparency (see [pricing models](/blog/white-label-exchange-pricing-models)).
- **Support & roadmap** — SLAs, integration help, and how fast they ship.

<Callout title="Watch the pricing black box">
Most providers don't publish exchange pricing — costs depend on deployment scope, customization, compliance and liquidity. Get itemised quotes and compare total cost of ownership (setup + revenue share + liquidity + support), not headline numbers. Insist on a reference call with an existing operator.
</Callout>

## Matching provider to situation

- **Multi-asset broker/exchange combo** → a multi-asset infrastructure vendor.
- **Fast branded launch on proven infra** → a turnkey provider running its own live venue.
- **Deep in-house engineering, want control** → an open-source or source-available stack.
- **Derivatives-first venue** → weight the risk engine, funding/mark-price design and derivatives liquidity above all else.

*Transparency note: GammaFloww — which publishes this guide — is itself a derivatives-focused white-label provider. We've deliberately left it out of the comparison above so you can weigh the field on its merits; evaluate us against the same criteria.*

## The takeaway

There is no single "best" white-label derivatives provider — only the best fit for your product, market and engineering capacity. Compare on engine, derivatives depth, liquidity, custody, compliance, pricing transparency and support, and always talk to a current operator before signing. Then sanity-check the [build-vs-buy](/blog/white-label-vs-building-crypto-exchange) decision itself.
